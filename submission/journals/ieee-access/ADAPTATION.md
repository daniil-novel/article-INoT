## Evidence safeguards

- The allocation is at most 1,000 sampled task units with three repeats, not 15,000 independent tasks. Two hundred task IDs overlap the earlier model study, and all outputs were regenerated.
- Role-label null hypotheses are not rejected after Holm correction; neither equivalence nor non-inferiority follows. The positive descriptive MR–MN interval and adjusted `p=0.0599` remain visible together.
- Quality and resources use their own eligible paired subsets. API-equivalent valuation is a counter-based quantity at disclosed historical rates, not a current bill or total cost.
- The topology treatment includes repeated context, intermediate responses, and additional output. It is not a pure call-count effect or an equal-compute comparison.
- The SCC study retains 396 Docker failures. Its conditional negative contrasts do not establish full-population inferiority because extreme bounds allow either sign.
- Directional agreement across two model aliases on reused tasks is corroboration, not evidence about independent model families.
