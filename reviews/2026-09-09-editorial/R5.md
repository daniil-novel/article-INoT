# R5 — text, bibliography, and integrity review

I read the complete 39-page English PDF (`converted_article_springer.pdf`, SHA-256 `bcccd4c757fcf4d5daebf597bd9fc92a75441bcc07d8670d06378be489bdf7e9`) and the active TeX snapshot `tmp/editorial-20260909/en-review-final.tex`, including all tables, figures, exact prompts, outcome appendices, historical evidence, declarations, and the 17-entry bibliography. This report does not review the other editorial reports.

The manuscript is unusually explicit about the evidentiary boundary. It consistently separates role labels from latent agents, native test outcomes from model observations, exploratory batches from the four primary tests, and API-equivalent valuation from an invoice. The abstract, Tables 3–11, conclusion, and appendices agree on the main denominators and on the central negative result: the observed single-call resource difference does not establish quality preservation or non-inferiority. Missing generations, control-ineligible tasks, timeout statuses, and the amended continuation are disclosed rather than silently converted into failures or successes.

The principal editorial risk is repetition around the same boundary conditions. “Not an invoice/subscription charge,” “not a quality-preservation certificate,” and “not evidence of hidden independent agents” recur in Methods, Results, Discussion, and appendices. These repetitions are substantively useful, but two or three could be shortened by pointing back to the first formal definition. The terms “role-labelled,” “internal roles,” and “agents” are generally controlled well; retain the qualification that roles are instructed functions whenever the shorter “roles” appears in the abstract or conclusion.

One material traceability point needs author attention. The final manuscript identifies the evidence archive as commit `96be37745b7f` (p. 28 / active TeX line 671), while the supplied final manuscript snapshot is commit `823ac21`. Both commits exist locally, so this is not a broken citation; however, the wording should make clear that `96be37745b7f` is the frozen evidence snapshot and `823ac21` is the manuscript snapshot. Otherwise readers may mistake the two identities.
