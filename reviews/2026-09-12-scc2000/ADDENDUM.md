# Addendum to the SCC-2000 launch review

The first blocker in `LAUNCH_REVIEW.md` is withdrawn after inspecting the actual frozen dispatcher lock. `scc_dispatch.dispatch_lock()` opens `out/DISPATCH.lock` exclusively and holds it for the complete dispatch context. The historical lock remains in place throughout `continuation.recover()`'s archive copy and status rewrite, so a second frozen dispatcher cannot acquire the study lock and begin work during that mutation window. The prior review treated this as an unprotected race; that was too strong. The existing recovery tests and the subsequent all-byte validation provide adequate evidence for the practical recovery exclusion in this workflow.

The watcher concern is addressed by the current adapter. `require_fresh_heartbeat()` rejects `PAUSED.json`, a missing or malformed heartbeat, `allow_model_work != true`, a reserve threshold other than 65, remaining reserve `<=65`, and a heartbeat older than 75 seconds (continuation.py:153–162). `selection_adapter()` calls this check for every selected payload immediately before it is submitted to the unchanged coordinator (continuation.py:166–181), while `validate_generate()` still requires the initial live watcher PID/module/cwd/creation match and a fresh strict-65 guard (continuation.py:232–253). With the stated 30-second watcher interval, this is a defensible bounded heartbeat invariant for launch; it need not claim a mathematically atomic OS lease. A pause or stale heartbeat rejects the next selected submission and preserves the frozen worker and no-retry policy.

The offline test suite currently passes **27 tests** across the SCC-2000 continuation, finish, and analysis files. The tests cover prefix ordering and malformed blocks, selected-only adapter behavior, recovery liveness rejection, byte preservation and tamper detection, and analysis/finish contracts. No model/native call or benchmark outcome was inspected.

One launch prerequisite remains material: `validate_generate()` requires the selection manifest, amendment, adapter, recovery manifest, and authorization file to be tracked and clean relative to `HEAD` (continuation.py:240–245). Therefore the final freeze-v3 files and corrected sources must be committed before launch, or the preflight will reject the intentionally uncommitted state. This is a repository-state prerequisite, not a statistical or runtime defect. After that commit, the corrected launch review has no remaining blocker demonstrated by the read-only checks performed here, subject to the separate live reserve watcher and root's final offline audit.
