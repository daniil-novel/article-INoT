## One wording caveat

The statement in `standalone_details_en.tex:9` that the unchanged evaluator “imposes its larger task timeout of 241 seconds” is supported by the recorded native run metadata (`effective_test_timeout_seconds=241`, `upstream_timeout_floor_seconds=240`) and is distinct from the 0.1-second `min_time_limit` CLI argument. For maximal self-containment, the sentence could call this the *effective per-test timeout recorded by the evaluator* rather than imply that 241 is a literal argument in the local wrapper. This is a precision improvement, not a substantive error.

The statements distinguishing generation unavailability from native failure, and primary 180-second calls from expanded-study 600-second calls, are correct. No numerical error, nonexistent arm, or overclaim of standalone reproducibility was found in the inspected additions.
