\subsection{Source overlap in the expanded allocation}
An additional source-only audit, specified during the Luna extension described in Appendix~\ref{app:expanded-studies}, examines all 1,140 source tasks and its fixed 1,000-task allocation. It uses no generated programs or outcome records. All selected instructions are distinct after whitespace normalization. Exact reference-program abstract syntax tree (AST) comparison removes docstrings but preserves names, literals and structure. Instruction similarity is the Jaccard coefficient of sets of five consecutive words after case folding and removal of the starter-code paragraph. A near-code link requires at least 20 identifier occurrences in each program, set Jaccard similarity of at least 0.80 and multiset Jaccard similarity of at least 0.70 over non-keyword identifiers and string/number literals, preserving their spelling. This is a specified adaptation of token-based duplicate screening~\cite{allamanis2019duplication}, not a test of semantic equivalence.

The union of instruction Jaccard similarity at least 0.70, near-code links and exact matches yields 992 connected groups among the allocated tasks: seven groups contain 15 tasks, and the largest contains three. Instruction-only thresholds 0.50, 0.70 and 0.90 yield 996, 999 and 1,000 groups. These are screening partitions, not estimates of independent semantic units. In particular, /130 and /131 share the same reference AST. Across allocations, /1120 shares its instruction and reference AST with /1121 in the separate 80-task experiment; /826 shares its reference AST with development task /814. Distinct IDs therefore do not guarantee source separation across studies.

Connected components are formed over all 1,140 source tasks and then intersected with the assigned sample. The seven non-singleton groups under the union rule are \{/1097,/1099\}, \{/130,/131\}, \{/349,/351,/353\}, \{/369,/622\}, \{/401,/413\}, \{/423,/426\} and \{/673,/675\}. No task is removed. The supplementary analysis will first average three repeated differences within task, then resample whole source groups 10,000 times with seed 20260911. Each draw divides the sum of sampled task differences by the sampled task count, preserving the task-weighted estimand when group sizes differ. The resulting 95\% percentile intervals are conditional on the chosen graph and remain separate from the registered tests. Unequal group sizes and unrecognized dependence limit their interpretation~\cite{mackinnon2023clusters}. Source comparisons are complete; outcome-based intervals will be computed after the Luna extension is complete.
