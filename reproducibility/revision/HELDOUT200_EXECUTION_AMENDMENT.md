# Administrative continuation of never-submitted assignments

8 September 2026. Amendment recorded after the first held-out shard reported
one 180-second CLI timeout, before any held-out model output was tested or
quality results were inspected. The original protocol and first attempt are
immutable and remain labelled partial. Counts and transport status were
inspected; no task was selected by answer quality.

The first plan stops an entire shard after one failed cell, leaving many
unrelated randomized assignments unsubmitted. To complete the intended sample
without repeating a model attempt, a separate continuation may submit only
cells for which the original archive contains **no submitted turn at all**.
Any cell with a started turn is permanently ineligible for continuation,
including a multi-call cell that failed before its final stage. No successful
or failed response is regenerated, replaced, repaired, or selected between
alternatives. The exact frozen task, condition, replicate label, prompt builder,
full history policy, CLI binary, model, medium reasoning and 180-second limit
remain unchanged. There is no compression or tool feedback.

Wait until all original shards have terminated, then hash their complete
assignment/turn inventory and freeze the exact never-submitted cell list and
continuation source before dispatch. Keep their original within-shard order;
record interleaving and continuation timing. A timeout in this continuation
ends that cell and leaves its final quality unavailable; other never-submitted
assignments continue. A quota/authentication failure stops the continuation.
At most eight experimental CLI processes may be active across all batches.
The continuation has no automatic retry and cannot overwrite an archive.

The first-attempt archive, continuation archive, submitted-turn costs and
unknown usage are reported separately and jointly. Report all 1,000 assigned
cells, generation availability, original native test outcomes, evaluator
eligibility, complete-pair contrasts and all-assignment missingness bounds.
Do not count an absent program as an observed native test failure. An optional
end-to-end delivery rate may count generation failures as failures, with an
explicitly different denominator and label. The original four contrasts remain
fixed; execution under an amended protocol must be disclosed beside their
estimates and p-values. This is not an unmodified preregistered execution.

changing the INoT treatment, adding tasks, changing the response deadline,
