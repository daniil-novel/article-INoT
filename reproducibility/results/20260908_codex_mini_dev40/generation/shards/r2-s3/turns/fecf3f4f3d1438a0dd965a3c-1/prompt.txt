3. **Convert DataFrame to CSV**

2. **Lists of unequal length**
   - Pandas will fill missing values with `NaN`.
   - CSV output will include empty cells for missing values.

3. **Non-list values**

5. **Nested objects or complex types**
   - Objects like dicts or lists inside cells may be stringified in CSV.
   - The function should not try to flatten them unless required.

6. **Special characters / Unicode**
